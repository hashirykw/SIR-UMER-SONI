Step 1 of 4

Draw it.

Every topic opens on the structure itself. Students draw the outline before a single term is introduced, so the shape is already in their head.

Step 2 of 4

Label lines go to the exact part, with the exact name the mark scheme uses. Most Paper 2 diagrams lose marks here, not on the drawing.

Step 3 of 4

Say what it does.

Chloroplast: contains chlorophyll, which absorbs light energy for photosynthesis. Structure plus function is the line that earns the second mark.
