Muhammad Umer Soni

O Level & IGCSE Biology · diagram first

Sir Umer Soni teaches O Level Biology 5090 and IGCSE Biology 0610 in Karachi. In his classes the diagram comes first and the definition comes after it, because both papers give more marks to a student who can label a structure and say what it does than to one who can recite the textbook line about it.

He has simplified Biology for students since day one, with exam-focused lessons and A* strategies. Four Cambridge distinctions are on his record so far, and 18,000 students follow his lessons on Instagram.
